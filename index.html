Expert Insights from My Field
What the Experts Say About Success in Cybersecurity
  • 01
    Build a T-shaped skill set. The most effective cybersecurity professionals have broad foundational knowledge and deep expertise in at least one area. For operations professionals entering the field, existing domain knowledge is a significant advantage — pair it with technical depth.
  • 02
    Communication is as important as technical skill. The ability to translate complex security risks into business language — for boards, executives, and non-technical staff — is what separates good analysts from great ones. Operations experience is a direct pipeline to this skill.
  • 03
    Stay current and curious — the field never stops moving. Cybersecurity evolves daily. Successful professionals maintain a habit of continuous learning: following threat intelligence, earning progressive certifications, and participating in communities. Curiosity is not optional — it is the job.
